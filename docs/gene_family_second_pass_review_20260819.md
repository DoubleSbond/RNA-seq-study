The GST review candidates have homology/domain evidence for CLIC, RNA-binding,
thioredoxin, prostaglandin E synthase, or metaxin-like proteins rather than
canonical cytosolic detoxification GSTs. The UGT review candidates lack
PF00201/IPR002213 and show alternative architectures. The two SULT review
candidates are non-cytosolic sulfotransferase classes.

ABC candidates require architecture-aware interpretation. One historical
candidate is an ABCF-family soluble ATPase and should be retained despite the
absence of a transmembrane domain. Six historical partial candidates are kept
as likely ABC fragments; ten additional partial candidates and one historical
domain-negative candidate remain on hold for isoform/homology inspection.
Only four domain-negative, non-historical entries are suggested for exclusion
from the current rough set.

These recommendations define a working set, not final nomenclature. The
original broad and domain-screened tables remain the authoritative audit trail.
