# unknownCYP final-round summary

## Source definition

The Phoenei_unmatched sheet contains 33 CYP-related entries. Among them, 24 entries were labeled as CYP_unknown and were used as the source set for the unknownCYP recheck. The remaining 9 entries already had recovered CYP annotations, such as CYP6AE21, CYP6B7, CYP6A2, CYP6J1, and CYP304A1, and were therefore treated as annotated CYPs rather than unknownCYP candidates.

## Recheck strategy

The 24 Phoenei CYP_unknown candidates were re-evaluated using:
1. expression level,
2. conserved CYP motif completeness,
3. ORF / peptide length,
4. Bmori and Spodoptera homolog information,
5. diagnostic phylogenetic placement.

## Key candidates

DN598 is the strongest unknownCYP candidate because it combines high expression, complete CYP motifs, and CYP6B-like phylogenetic placement.

DN2810 and DN414 copies are additional CYP6B-related candidates with moderate expression.

DN6330 is a CYP6B/6AE-like candidate with good sequence evidence but low expression.

DN2768 is a CYP6K1-like supportive candidate with Dan-biased and replicate-variable expression.

DN6927 and DN17545 represent non-CYP6 candidates, placed as CYP18A1-like and CYP4-like respectively.

DN1031, DN3806, and DN420 showed high TPM values but were not prioritized because of insufficient motif, ORF, or phylogenetic support.

DN521 was retained for transparency but is likely non-CYP/transposon-related and should be excluded from CYP candidate interpretation.

## Main conclusion

Among the 24 original Phoenei CYP_unknown candidates, DN598 is the best candidate novel Ph CYP6B-like sequence. The result supports the presence of previously unresolved CYP6-related candidates in P. hoenei, while also showing that high TPM alone is insufficient for CYP candidate prioritization.
