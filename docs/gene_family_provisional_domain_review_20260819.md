## Rules used

- CarE: PF00135 or IPR002018.
- GST: cytosolic GST-related N/C-terminal Pfam or InterPro evidence retained as
  provisional; CLIC and other GST-fold proteins still require annotation and
  homology review.
- UGT: PF00201 or IPR002213.
- SULT: PF00685 or IPR000863.
- ABC: both NBD evidence (PF00005/IPR003439) and TMD evidence
  (PF00664/PF01061/IPR011527/IPR013525) for provisional HQ.

These are screening rules, not final nomenclature or orthology criteria.
Transcript completeness, peptide length, representative isoform, top-hit
annotation, and family-specific motifs remain required for final HQ calls.
